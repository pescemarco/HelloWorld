## Bio

I was born on April 25th 1974 in Nizza Monferrato, a small town in the South of Piemonte, a district where agriculture and small but highly specialized mechanical factories still cohexist side by side. 

When I was a kid, my father gave me and my bro a computer. It was a Commodore64, featuring 64kB RAM (of which about 38kB) available for the user's programs), a datassete tape recorder to save and load programs, a RF TV output connector, plus a parallel port, a cartridge slot and a pair of dsub-9 connector to mate a couple of joystick. How many fun hours we had
It came with a programmer's manual explaining in a certain details its BASIC v2 language. And many other hours of fun came from there.

Life was flowing quiet and overall nicely, meeting/playing/chatting (face to face) with friends were the main activities after school, in the home court or at the field of the church.

One of the things I was thinking about at that time was how to build automation of our scale electric train, which was a bit out of the reach for me with just my Commodore and no internet.
So I turned to drawing sketches of cars and engines on the side of my note book during the Literature lessons.

Then, the choice for Mechanical Engineering was almost obviuos, though a strong inclination for coding and automating any kind of calculation was still strong inside me.

If the master degree in 1999 was a major milestone, getting hired at the Fiat Research Centre in 2000 was the begin of an extraordinary experience that gave me the opportunity to learn vehicle dynamics through track testing, simulation, developing data processing algorithms for objective performance evaluation, applying and refining product development methodologies and, most of all, working side by side with a bunch of  wonderful people and among them some of the greatest experts in the chassis and vehicle dynamics domain.
